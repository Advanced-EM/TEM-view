TEM-view Console

The hand panels of a transmission electron microscope, sized for a desk, with a voice. Focus, stigmate and drive the stage of the Seeing with Electrons exhibit with real knobs, or just ask it.

Drag to orbit · turn a knob · click a key · hold the mic key for a scripted voice demo

TEM · 200 kV · Δf −12.0 nm

Every control has a job on the column

Each knob maps to the control it stands for on a real microscope, and what it does follows the operating mode, as on a manufacturer's console.

Focus

A focus dial with real resolution

The 58 mm optical encoder moves defocus by 0.2 nm per detent with the aberration corrector on, and 1 nm without. Press it to switch between fine and coarse.

Stage and stigmation

The stage under your thumb

A Hall-effect joystick drives the stage. Two multifunction knobs set the stigmator, image shift or tilt as an X/Y vector, and in the Ronchigram the corrector's coma, three-fold and four-fold terms.

Voice

Ask the microscope

Hold the mic key and say "defocus minus fifty", "switch to the Ronchigram" or "what am I looking at?". Two MEMS microphones pick up your voice; the exhibit answers with its narration and moves the controls for you.

Privacy

A mute you can trust

The microphones only stream while the key is held, and a hardware switch on the back cuts their power. When it is off, the status light turns red and no software can listen. Made for classrooms and museum floors.

Status

Lights that read the column

The ring around the focus dial takes the color of the operating mode. Keys light while the wobbler runs or the beam is blanked, a blue light line runs along the front edge, and a 128 × 32 display shows the mode, the voltage and the last change.

USB-C, no drivers

The console is a standard USB device: game controller and microphone in one. Any browser with the Gamepad API drives the exhibit as soon as it is plugged in, and the microphone appears like any USB headset; Chrome and Edge also send the lights and display back over WebHID. Every knob and key can be remapped in the exhibit's panel dialog, and the firmware is open.

Target specifications

For the production run. The prototype uses a 3D-printed body; the numbers will be confirmed with manufacturing quotes before the campaign.

Size
210 × 120 × 34 mm, about 660 g
Body
Smoked frosted polycarbonate top, CNC 6063 aluminum base, bead-blasted and anodized black, blue light-pipe edge
Controls
58 mm optical focus dial with push (fine/coarse) · 5 encoders with push (magnification, intensity, context, multifunction X and Y) · Hall-effect joystick · 8 PBT keys with blue legends
Voice
2 MEMS microphones behind acoustic mesh · hold-to-talk key · hardware mute switch with red status light
Feedback
RGB status ring, per-key lighting and front light line · 128 × 32 OLED
Electronics
RP2350, USB-C, composite USB device: HID (8 axes, 16 buttons) and USB audio
Works with
Any current browser on Windows, macOS, Linux and ChromeOS; lights and display need Chrome or Edge
License
Open hardware and firmware
