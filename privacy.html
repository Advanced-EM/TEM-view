Privacy

The exhibits on this site are TEM-view, Ruska 1933 and Scanning 1938–2002. They count how they are used, anonymously, so we can see which parts help people learn and where in the world they are used. This page says exactly what is counted, what is not, and how to turn it off.

Checking…

Who collects it

Roberto dos Reis, the author of TEM-view, collects and keeps these records. Contact: roberto.reis@northwestern.edu.

What is counted

Each time you open an exhibit page, it keeps one small record of that visit. It sends the record a minute after the page opens, then about once a minute while you use it, and when you leave. The record holds:

  • which exhibit, the time it was opened (to the minute), and how long it was open and in view;
  • how long each mode, specimen, instrument or mission was on screen;
  • how many times features were used: acquisitions, photographic plates, training tasks, Python scripting, voice and AI commands, the control panel, structure files you loaded (only the fact that one was loaded, never its contents);
  • the kinds of input used (mouse, touch, keyboard, game controller, control panel);
  • the device class (phone, tablet or desktop), the window size rounded to 100 pixels, the browser language, and the website that linked to the exhibit, if any;
  • a random id for that one page visit, so that its later reports replace the earlier ones.

When the record arrives, the collector adds the approximate location that our network provider, Cloudflare, derives from the connection: country, region, city, time zone and coordinates rounded to about 10 km. To tell how many different people visit in a day, it also adds a visitor key. The key is a one-way code made from the connection address, a secret and the date. It changes every day and cannot be turned back into an address.

What is not

  • No IP address is stored. It is used only for the moment it takes to work out the city and the daily visitor key.
  • No cookies, no advertising, no tracking across other sites, and no device fingerprinting.
  • Nothing you type, say or record: commands, voice, scripts, images and structure files stay in your browser.
  • No name, email or account. The records cannot be linked to you.
  • TEM-view Kids (Electron Explorer) sends nothing. On a museum kiosk it keeps anonymous daily totals for the museum (visits, time spent, missions, pictures, stickers printed, languages) on that device only; they never leave it. Its take-home QR code holds only the picture's settings: no name, time, place or photo.

Where it goes

CollectorA Cloudflare Worker receives the records and keeps them in a Cloudflare database for up to 90 days.
DatasetOnce a day, the previous day's records are added to a private Hugging Face dataset belonging to Roberto dos Reis. They are used for usage statistics: how many visits, from which cities and countries, which parts are used and for how long. Only totals and maps made from them may be shown publicly, never single records.
Not sharedThe records are not sold or given to anyone else.

Turning it off

  • Use the button at the top of this page, or Turn off on the notice the exhibit shows the first time. It is remembered in this browser.
  • If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, nothing is counted.
  • Add ?notrack to an exhibit's address, for example on a museum kiosk.

Other data

  • Training sessions: during a training task you can choose to record your session. Record only keeps it in your browser. Record and share sends it, with an anonymous id and the experience level you chose, to a private research dataset on alignment.
  • News: if you subscribe, your name, email, role and optional institution are kept privately to send you news, and nowhere else. Ask to be removed at any time.
  • Hosting: the site is served by GitHub Pages, which receives your IP address when it delivers the files, like any website; its own privacy policy applies. The fonts and the 3D library are served from this site. The Python editor, when you open it, loads Pyodide and CodeMirror from jsDelivr and cdnjs.

Your rights

The usage records are anonymous: they carry no IP address or identifier that leads back to you, so we cannot find or delete records about a particular person. You can stop them at any time as described above. For questions, write to roberto.reis@northwestern.edu or open an issue on GitHub.

Kurz auf Deutsch

Die Ausstellungen zählen ihre Nutzung anonym: welche Ausstellung, welche Teile wie lange genutzt werden, Geräteart und Sprache, dazu die ungefähre Stadt, die Cloudflare aus der Verbindung ableitet. Die IP-Adresse wird nicht gespeichert, es gibt keine Cookies und keine Verfolgung über andere Seiten. TEM-view Kids sendet nichts; an einem Museumskiosk zählt es anonyme Tagessummen nur auf diesem Gerät. Verantwortlich: Roberto dos Reis (roberto.reis@northwestern.edu). Die Daten liegen bis zu 90 Tage bei Cloudflare und danach in einem privaten Hugging-Face-Datensatz für Nutzungsstatistiken. Ausschalten: mit der Schaltfläche oben, mit „Do Not Track“ oder „Global Privacy Control“ im Browser, oder mit ?notrack in der Adresse.
