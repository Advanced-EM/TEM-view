## Description

Drive a transmission electron microscope in your pocket. TEM-view simulates what a real instrument records:
- **Physics, not pictures:** every image is computed from the physics of an electron wave passing through a real
  arrangement of atoms.
- **Three modes:**
  - TEM: phase contrast, Thon rings and focus
  - STEM: a probe scanned across atomic columns, with bright-field, annular and HAADF detectors
  - The Ronchigram: the pattern microscopists use to tune an aberration corrector
- **Training tasks with a hidden answer:** as on a real instrument, the knob readings do not show the true optics.
  Focus and stigmate a gold specimen, run the direct alignments from the gun to the objective stigmator, or tune the
  corrector until the Ronchigram's flat center opens up. Then rate your result before the app reveals the truth.
- **Six specimens:** gold nanoparticles, silicon, a strontium titanate grain boundary, graphene, a metal-organic
  framework, and proteins in ice.
- **Voice commands:** hold the talk button and say "switch to STEM", "defocus minus fifty" or "load graphene".
- **Works offline:** no account, no ads.
- **Your data stays yours:** if you choose to share a training session, it is sent anonymously to help research
  how people align microscopes.

From the Seeing with Electrons exhibit. The full version with ten modes, diffraction, spectroscopy, holography and
Python scripting runs free in any browser at advanced-em.github.io/TEM-view.
